## 3. Analysis & Direction
B4's query competition is the strongest complete result. G1's direct
mask-quality targets, C1's listwise score and H5's all-query message did
not improve it. W1 found penultimate mask loss in 69/1660 training
expressions but only 23/388 material selection errors; W3's frozen
quality product reduced train64 mIoU. W2 is a separate data-to-graph
mechanism: true counts versus rotated counts show a nonzero controlled
response and a native trainable path, despite almost unchanged frozen
top-1. No ablation or numeric sweep is being smuggled into the queue.

superpoints, not GT labels. Train/validation split and 30 epochs remain
unchanged; no checkpoint cherry-picking beyond the native best-mIoU rule.
Compare B4-E30 0.5094, B1 0.5020 and target 0.5150. A historical
epoch-20 continuation is not a fresh matched B4 run; if W2 wins, a
fresh B4 repetition is necessary to attribute gain. Scene is the paired
bootstrap/cluster unit; noise floor unquantified until independent repeat.
Material-harm subgroup rule: at least 20 scenes and scene-cluster interval
wholly below zero triggers diagnosis; no single-scene veto. Record full
latency, active queries and training/inference memory.

## 7. Risk Assessment
The fixed train64 W2 selector changed only 6/1660 predictions, none of the
flips beneficial and one harmful; training may be null or adverse. This is
not a validation result. Soft overlap may provide only weak additional
gradients after B4 training, though batch `[256,257]` shows nonzero
layer/readout/gradient differences. GPU peak 6.434 GiB is a two-batch
measurement, not full-run bound; monitor OOMs. No guaranteed theoretical
novelty or >0.515. Three crash repair attempts maximum, then close.

## 8. Smell Check
This is not a weight, temperature, threshold, top-k or seed scan: a
query-pair interaction takes new observed point-mass input. No new learnable
parameter, but the causal information path is non-elementwise (point-mass
pairwise overlap). Same B4 config values avoid numeric selection; the
candidate tests whether that representation matters at all. The
correspondence-breaking rotated-count control is read-only diagnosis,
not an additional queued variant.
