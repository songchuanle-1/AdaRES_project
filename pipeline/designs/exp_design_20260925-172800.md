## 3. Analysis & Direction
The first decoder query self-attention reaches 128 nearest queries, but
the last reaches four. A final all-query message after the local path
makes distant candidate information available to the last mask and
competition indicator. Fixed subset: 215/240 material-error selected/oracle
pairs are mutually outside final four; this is access diagnosis only.
The change is not D1's learned pairwise confidence weighting.

global message may be redundant or destabilizing. B4-E30 is a historical
continuation, not a fresh seed-matched run. Native probes do not bound
maximum memory or predict trained mIoU.

numerical configuration or renamed D1 pair-score head. The number of
additional layers is fixed to one final layer from the access diagnosis,
not scanned. No novelty claim is made.
